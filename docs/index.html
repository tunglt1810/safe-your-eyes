Safe Your Eyes

I built this because working with AI made me forget to look away from the screen. Every so often it asks you to close your eyes for a bit. Then you get back to work.

Download

Breaks that stay out of the way

15 seconds every 15 minutes, 60 seconds every 75 minutes. A notice comes first, and a 2-second key lock stops accidental skips.

Knows when to wait

It pauses when you are idle and holds off during fullscreen apps or on battery. Strict mode is there if you want it.

0% GPU while idle

One binary, no Tokio. The UI only starts for a break or when you open Settings.

No admin rights

Everything lives in your user account, on every OS.

Settings

Change the timings and the rules from a native window. It follows your system theme.
